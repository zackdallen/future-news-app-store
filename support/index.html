For help with Farlight News, email Zack Dallen. If something isn’t working, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were trying to do.

The edition won’t load

Check your internet connection, then reopen the app. You can also open the web edition in your browser.

How do I check a forecast?

Tap the story to see its sources and formula. Open Results to see how previous forecasts performed.

Where are my settings and calls saved?

They stay on the device where you made them. See the privacy policy for details.
