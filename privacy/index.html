Privacy policy

You can read Farlight News without creating an account. The app has no sign-in, comments, location requests, ads, or in-app analytics.

On your device

News category preferences, your forecast calls, and haptic settings stay on your device. Farlight News does not transmit these settings or guesses to its servers. Clear the site’s data in your browser or delete the iPhone app to remove them.

Loading the news

Hosting providers process standard network details, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect the app and website. The web edition’s host uses a security cookie to help block automated abuse. Farlight News does not use requests or this cookie to build reader profiles. The news archive contains original summaries of public records and forecast data.

Links and support

When you open a source link, the destination site’s privacy policy applies. If you choose to email support, we use your email address and the information you provide to reply and address your request.
